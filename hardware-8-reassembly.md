# Part 8: Reassembly

With all the Kratos components installed and connected, it is worth proving the console works before it goes back together.

## Step 1: Double-Check Every Connection

Work around everything you have connected and make sure none of it is loose or trapped:

- The 9-pin front panel cable into the motherboard header
- The RGB cables between the Kratos Main Board and each Controller Board
- The 5V standby wire, if you fitted one
- The SMBus SDA and SCL wires

## Step 2: Power Up and Check the Front Panel

With the console still open, reconnect the power lead and turn the Xbox on. The front panel lights should come on.

**Take care with the console open** - the power supply is exposed, so keep your hands and any tools clear of it while the Xbox is plugged in.

If nothing lights up, power off, unplug, and recheck the connections from Step 1 before going any further.

Once you are happy, power the console off and unplug it again.

## Step 3: Refit the Drives

This is the reverse of Part 2:

1. Lower the DVD drive back into place and reconnect its IDE cable and the yellow cable to the motherboard
2. Refit the two T10 screws at the front of the DVD drive - they are far easier to reach with the hard drive still out
3. Lower the hard drive carrier back in, seat the power cord in the track on the drive tray, and reconnect its IDE cable
4. Refit the last T10 screw, the one that tucks under the IDE cable

## Step 4: Close the Console Up

1. Lower the top shell back on, watching the internal wires as it goes down
2. Refit the six T20 case screws
3. Press the rubber feet back down over the four screws they cover
4. Reconnect all your cables
