# Hardware Installation

This guide will walk you through the physical installation of your Kratos front button panel replacement.

## Prerequisites

- Kratos kit with all components
- Compatible original Xbox
- Anti-static precautions (recommended)

### Tools Needed

- **Torx screwdrivers** - T10, T15, and T20
- **Spudger or tweezers** - For prying open components
- **Flathead screwdriver** - For removing rubber feet and separating shells
- **Soldering iron and solder** - For the controller port shielding and the front panel connector
- **Desoldering braid or solder sucker** - For removing the 9-pin connector
- **Flux** - Makes desoldering and resoldering much easier
- **Isopropyl alcohol and a soft brush** - For cleaning off flux residue

## Installation Parts

The hardware installation is split into the following parts. Work through them in order:

1. **[Part 1: Opening Your Xbox](hardware-1-opening-your-xbox.md)** - Open the console and remove the drives to access the front panel
2. **[Part 2: Removing the Original Front Panel](hardware-2-removing-the-original-front-panel.md)** - Remove the front bezel to reach the stock front button panel board
3. **[Part 3: Preparing the Controller Ports](hardware-3-preparing-the-controller-ports.md)** - Remove the original controller ports and their shielding
4. **[Part 4: Preparing the Kratos Main Board](hardware-4-installing-the-kratos-main-board.md)** - Transfer the 9-pin connector from the original board to the Kratos Main Board
5. **[Part 5: Installing the Controller Boards](hardware-5-installing-the-controller-boards.md)** - Fit the Kratos Controller Boards in the controller port locations
6. **[Part 6: Connecting the Modxo Harness](hardware-6-connecting-the-modxo-harness.md)** - Connect Kratos to your Modxo board
7. **[Part 7: Reassembly](hardware-7-reassembly.md)** - Put the console back together

Start with [Part 1: Opening Your Xbox](hardware-1-opening-your-xbox.md).
