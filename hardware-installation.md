# Hardware Installation

This guide will walk you through the physical installation of your Kratos front button panel replacement.

## Prerequisites

- Kratos kit with all components
- Compatible original Xbox
- Anti-static precautions (recommended)

### Tools Needed

- **Torx screwdrivers** - T10, T15, and T20
- **Spudger or tweezers** - For prying open components
- **Flathead screwdriver** - For removing rubber feet and separating shells
- **Soldering iron and solder** - For the controller port shielding and the front panel connector
- **Desoldering braid or solder sucker** - For removing the 9-pin connector
- **Flux** - Makes desoldering and resoldering much easier
- **Isopropyl alcohol and a soft brush** - For cleaning off flux residue

## Installation Parts

The hardware installation is split into the following parts. Work through them in order:

1. **[Part 1: Before You Start](hardware-1-before-you-start.md)** - How this install approach works, and optionally getting Kratos onto your WiFi over USB-C first
2. **[Part 2: Opening Your Xbox](hardware-2-opening-your-xbox.md)** - Open the console and remove the drives to access the front panel
3. **[Part 3: Removing the Original Front Panel](hardware-3-removing-the-original-front-panel.md)** - Remove the front bezel to reach the stock front button panel board
4. **[Part 4: Preparing the Controller Ports](hardware-4-preparing-the-controller-ports.md)** - Remove the original controller ports and their shielding, then fit the Kratos Controller Boards
5. **[Part 5: Preparing the Kratos Main Board](hardware-5-preparing-the-kratos-main-board.md)** - Transfer the 9-pin connector from the original board to the Kratos Main Board, then fit the cables
6. **[Part 6: Refitting the Front Panel](hardware-6-refitting-the-front-panel.md)** - Route the cables and clip the front panel back onto the console
   - **[Part 6a: Xbox 1.0 to 1.5](hardware-6a-refitting-the-front-panel-1-0-1-5.md)** - Route the cable and clip the panel on, plus optional standby power for wireless power-on
   - **[Part 6b: Xbox 1.6](hardware-6b-refitting-the-front-panel-1-6.md)** - 5V standby wiring for 1.6 consoles
7. **[Part 7: Connecting Kratos to the SMBus](hardware-7-connecting-kratos-to-the-smbus.md)** - Wire the SDA and SCL lines to the console's SMBus
8. **[Part 8: Reassembly](hardware-8-reassembly.md)** - Put the console back together

Start with [Part 1: Before You Start](hardware-1-before-you-start.md).
