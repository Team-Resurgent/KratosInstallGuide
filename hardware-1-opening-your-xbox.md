# Part 1: Opening Your Xbox

Before the Kratos boards can be installed, the Xbox needs to be opened up and the drives removed so the front panel is accessible.

## Step 1: Prepare the Console

1. Work in a clean, well-lit area, and use an anti-static mat if available
2. Disconnect all cables from the Xbox
3. Place the console on a flat, stable surface
4. Organize your Kratos components and tools

## Step 2: Remove the Rubber Feet

Flip the Xbox upside down and carefully pry off the rubber feet using a spudger or flathead screwdriver. This exposes the screws hidden underneath.

## Step 3: Unscrew the Bottom Shell

Using a T20 Torx screwdriver, remove the four screws located under the rubber feet, plus the two additional screws hidden under the stickers.

**Note:** Removing the screws under the stickers may void any remaining warranty.

## Step 4: Lift Off the Top Shell

Turn the Xbox right-side-up and carefully lift off the top shell. Be cautious of the internal components and fragile wires underneath.

## Step 5: Remove the DVD Drive

1. Disconnect the power and data cables from the DVD drive
2. Using a T10 Torx screwdriver, remove the screws holding the drive in place
3. Gently lift the drive out of the console

## Step 6: Remove the Hard Drive

1. Disconnect the power cable from the hard drive
2. Remove any screws securing the hard drive caddy
3. Lift the hard drive out of the console

Your Xbox is now open with the front panel accessible, ready for the next stage.
