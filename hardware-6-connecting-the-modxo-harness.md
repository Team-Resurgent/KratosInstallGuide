# Part 6: Connecting the Modxo Harness

The Modxo Harness links the Kratos Main Board to your Modxo board for ultimate mod control.

1. Connect the Modxo Harness to the Kratos Main Board
2. Route the harness to your Modxo board location
3. Connect to your Modxo board (if applicable)
